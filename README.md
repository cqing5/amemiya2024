# Replication of "Calculated Comparisons: Manufacturing Societal Causal Judgments by Implying Different Counterfactual Outcomes"

Stimuli, data, and analysis script for a replication of Study 2 of Amemiya et al. (2024). This experiment aims to explain disagreements in people’s causal judgments about societal events, proposing that one mechanism may be that people can be made to have different counterfactuals in mind via comparison cases, which can lead them to make opposing causal judgments. Study 1 established people’s sensitivity to comparison cases, and Study 2 tested whether
people are aware that they can use this approach to manipulate others’ causal judgments to
support a particular agenda.
